Hi! If you're someone who grew up before all these modern digital tools and now wants to make YouTube videos with your own voice-over, but finds video editing complicated, I made this for you.

Even though this project can currently make only basic videos, my goal is to help bridge the digital gap and make video creation easier for people who didn't grow up with this technology.

I will keep improving this project so that making a video won't feel like a difficult technical task. 💝

From a Gen Alpha to the people who deserve an easier way to create.
