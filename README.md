
# VFRAME !

## What is VFRAME and Why is VFRAME ?

**VFRAME is a basic video editor. Moreover than a video editor, the name "Video Maker" fits best.**

Some of our senior citizens love to make YouTube videos.

Especially videos with their own narration.

But due to complicated editing stuff, most of 'em drop their wish off.

Since I know some of them, here is what I found:

- They don't need videos like ultra 8K edits.
- All they need is to make videos and images to form a simple video.
- They also love to make audio narratives.
- Editing softwares are complicated.
