What it does

Times itself to your audio

Add your narration and Vframe stretches or trims your photos and clips so the video lands exactly on your audio's length.

Photos and video clips together

Mix still photos with short video clips in one timeline — no editing timeline to fight with, just drop them in order.

Everything stays local

Vframe processes on your own computer. There's no server involved, so there's nothing to upload and nothing to wait on.

How it works

1

Install Vframe and open it — no account needed.

2

Add your narration, a first and last photo, and anything in between.

3

Press generate — Vframe builds and previews the video for you.

4

Download the finished .mp4 and share it however you like.

Why a desktop app?

Vframe started as a website, but keeping a server running to process everyone's videos costs money. Running it on your own computer means Vframe stays free, with no upload wait and no limits on file size.
